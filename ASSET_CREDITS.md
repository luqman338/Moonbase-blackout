- Source: NASA CCMC DONKI, [SEP record 30717](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI/view/SEP/30717/-1) and the [dated SEP API query](https://api.nasa.gov/DONKI/SEP?startDate=2024-05-11&endDate=2024-05-11&api_key=DEMO_KEY). The stored retrieval date is November 14, 2026.

NASA content can include separately protected third-party material. NASA branding and endorsement rights are not granted.

These are archived spacecraft observations, not lunar radiation measurements, live forecasts or validation of the fictional shelter. NASA does not endorse this independent game. The project does not claim ownership of, or apply Apache 2.0 to, the underlying NASA records.

## Audio removal and current scope

At the project lead's direction, all five third-party sound effects and the playback system were removed on November 15, 2026. No audio is included or requested by this release. The previous audio-provenance and audio-license compatibility blocker is resolved by removal, not by establishing new rights to those recordings.

The game retains its original SVG artwork, favicon, CSS interface and visual effects. System fonts are used rather than bundled font files. Visual/interaction references and development tools, including ChatGPT and Codex, remain credited in Behind the Mission. No audio source credits are needed for the current asset inventory.

NASA source links and provenance remain intact. This inventory is not legal certification of overall Space Apps submission eligibility. Final browser validation and project-lead review are recorded in [RELEASE_REPORT.md](./RELEASE_REPORT.md).
